## Explanada del bastión — prompt exacto

```text
Use case: stylized-concept (semi-realistic realtime 3D game art direction).
Create a single production-quality concept of a playable third-person dark fantasy survivor game, NOT a poster or marketing splash art. Target dimensions 1536 x 864, landscape 16:9. Full-bleed game scene only, no browser/device frame, no title, no captions, no collage, no HUD for this art-direction comparison. Render with convincing semi-realistic realtime-game material quality, natural human proportions and grounded medieval architecture. Avoid cartoon/chibi, low-poly faceting, painterly brushwork and photoreal cinematic blur.
Hard user constraints: PC-first web 3D game, perspective camera BEHIND and slightly ABOVE the warrior, approximately 6 meters behind, looking forward into the environment. Never top-down or isometric. Whole warrior visible, bottom-center occupying about 22 percent of image height; a broad view of traversable ground, enemies and the fortress ahead. Design plausibly buildable as a restrained Three.js game with modular architecture and readable surfaces rather than a CGI movie.
Hero: same archetype in each direction, adult human warrior with natural proportions, practical MEDIUM armor: brown leather, visible chain mail and limited worn-steel shoulder/forearm plates, charcoal short cloth skirt, sturdy boots. Back three-quarter view, no shield, no giant plate suit, no oversized ornaments. One realistic hand-and-a-half sword with a restrained orange flame along the blade; hero clearly distinct from undead. Keep sword and hand anatomy coherent.
Setting: OUTSIDE a medieval castle-fortress, overcast daytime, damp grey stone, believable iron fittings, weeds, vegetation, muddy or stone paths, a few warm torches. Somber but fully readable: enough diffuse light to distinguish dark clothing from ground, distant haze only. No night, no blizzard, no glowing fantasy crystals, no futuristic structures. Ground is largely flat for gameplay, no platforming. Castle interior not shown as a large level.
Enemies: about ten undead medieval soldiers spread across the midground, some skeletal and some reanimated in deteriorated mail and helmets, desaturated cloth remnants, simple swords or spears. Natural proportions, individual readable silhouettes, no excessive gore. No other living heroes or party companions. Enemies must leave movement lanes; small groups, not an opaque mass.
Effects: restrained flame on sword and a few embers; no fullscreen explosions or motion blur. Foreground in focus. No magic UI buttons; all attacks are automatic.
Variation direction: Name: Explanada del bastión. Architectural character: a compact, believable fortress with SQUARE towers and a squat keep sits in the RIGHT background, behind a low curtain wall. LEFT midground: ruined timber-and-stone outbuildings of a former siege camp, collapsed awnings, a low palisade with open gaps. A wide almost level grassy muster field occupies the center, crossed by a broad muddy track. No ravine or bridge, no dense forest, no round-tower central gatehouse. The broad circular clearing makes the arena legible, but still feels like a place outside a working medieval castle. Camera is a practical third-person exploration camera far enough back to show more ground and hazards; whole warrior no more than one THIRD of image height at lower center, never a close-up. Undead form a loose arc approaching from ruined camp and wall side gate, realistic armor and bones. Palette pale desaturated limestone, smoke-grey clouds, brown earth, dry straw and dark green grass. Breaks in cloud cover give soft silvery diffuse daylight; still somber overcast, never sunny heroic fantasy. Subtle warm torch glow at wall and orange sword flame. One readable sweep of the flaming sword, fine restrained embers, no opaque magical wave. Architecture materially less ornate and more modular than a prestige cinematic environment; grounded semi-realistic game scene with clear negative space for moving around hordes.
```
